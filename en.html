Microsoft Teams training for small businesses

Your team has Teams.What's still missing is the collaborating.

Most small companies have had Microsoft Teams for years – and use a fraction of it. I show your team how it turns into real collaboration: information is findable instead of scattered across mailboxes, and handovers and onboarding take less time. Clearly, patiently, as equals.

01  Why — what drives me

Technology should take weight off people, not pile it on.

Too many teams work with tools nobody ever explained to them. That costs time every day — and it leaves people unsure about their own work.

I'm convinced that nobody is too little of a technical person. Usually all that is missing is someone who explains it calmly and clearly.

Sound familiar?

The tool is there. The collaboration isn't yet.

“We email files back and forth – even though we have Teams.”

Channels, shared files and co-authoring sit unused, because nobody showed how they work in day-to-day practice. Every round of emails costs time and creates one more version.

“Everyone does it differently, nobody finds anything.”

Without a shared structure, things grow wild. Handovers suffer most: anyone joining or taking over a project searches instead of working. A clear order makes knowledge findable instead of locking it in mailboxes.

“Half the team doesn't dare touch it.”

Uncertainty blocks people. With the right guidance, scepticism turns into routine – and you stop paying for licences that are only half used.

03  What — the services

Concept and training – from one hand.

First we plan together how the collaboration should work. Then I teach it to your team – with your projects, your processes, your questions. No off-the-shelf slide deck.

For that I need your core team: those who know the daily work best know where time is lost every day.

Not sure where to start?

Then we begin with a review: I look at your Teams environment and you receive a short written report – what works well, what gets in the way, what to tackle first. That shows which of the services fits.

04  About

I explain technology so that people can follow.

I'm Timo – an IT professional who enjoys teaching. I trained in systems engineering and went on to a Swiss higher diploma in IT. For more than ten years I have worked in IT, mostly in the Bern area: from support and system administration through to leading a team for the modern workplace.

Teaching came along and stayed. I taught IT at the gibb vocational school, I have guided apprentices through their education as a vocational trainer for years, and today I accompany people with very different levels of experience as a practical trainer. On top of that comes formal teacher training at the Swiss Federal University for Vocational Education – so that explaining things well is a craft, not a coincidence.

All of it has shown me how differently people learn, and how much a patient counterpart matters.
